Start with the sample and the decision.

A concise description of the material and the reason for the analysis is more useful than selecting an instrument without context.

  • Material, coating, device, residue, liquid, film, wafer, component, or sample type
  • Observed defect, contamination, variation, degradation, or performance question
  • Feature size, depth, concentration, or electrical range when known
  • Sample dimensions, quantity, handling limits, and whether destructive preparation is acceptable
  • Reference, control, or comparison samples
  • Decision the result must support and the required deliverable

Deliverables follow the project scope.

Depending on the project, deliverables may include images, raw data, measurements, interpretation, technical reports, and recommendations.
