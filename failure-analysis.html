Mechanical failure analysis

Organize the operating history and physical evidence, identify plausible failure mechanisms, and turn the findings into focused corrective work.

Scope an investigationEmail Nouvex

Work in this route

This route fits broken, worn, unstable, misaligned, or repeatedly underperforming mechanical systems where the next decision depends on understanding the cause.

  • Root-cause investigation
  • Fracture and wear review
  • Vibration and alignment review
  • Corrective design changes
  • Inspection criteria

Preserve the evidence

Useful inputs include photos, failed parts, drawings, maintenance and operating records, load changes, observed symptoms, previous repairs, and the sequence of events.

The investigation scope depends on the available evidence. Findings should distinguish observations, assumptions, likely mechanisms, and work still needed to confirm a cause.
