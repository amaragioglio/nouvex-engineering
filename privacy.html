Privacy policy

This policy explains how Nouvex Engineering LLC handles information submitted through this website.

Information collected

The Scope Engine may collect your name, company, work email, industry, project timeline, selected engineering route, project description, inquiry reference, source page, and basic technical request data such as browser user-agent information.

Please do not submit confidential, export-controlled, regulated, or sensitive personal information until appropriate handling terms are agreed.

How it is used

Nouvex uses inquiry information to understand the requested work, respond to the sender, maintain an inquiry record, prevent abuse, and operate and secure the website.

The website does not sell submitted inquiry information. The current measurement hooks do not set analytics cookies or send analytics events to an external provider unless a provider is added later.

Service providers

Website infrastructure and configured processors may include Vercel for hosting, Supabase for inquiry storage, and Resend for email delivery. These providers process information to provide their contracted services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and choices

Inquiry information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for project follow-up, business records, security, and legal obligations. Retention may vary with the nature of the inquiry and any resulting engagement.

To ask about your information, request correction or deletion where applicable, or raise a privacy concern, email info@nouvexeng.com. Nouvex may need to verify the request before acting.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
