Privacy policy.

This policy explains how Nouvex Engineering LLC handles information submitted through this website.

Information collected

The inquiry form may collect your name, company, work email, industry, timeline, selected inquiry path, project description, inquiry reference, source page, and basic technical request data such as browser user-agent information.

Please do not submit confidential, export-controlled, regulated, or sensitive personal information until appropriate handling terms are agreed.

How information is used

Nouvex uses inquiry information to understand the requested work, respond to the sender, maintain an inquiry record, prevent abuse, and operate and secure the website.

The website does not sell submitted inquiry information. The current measurement hooks do not set analytics cookies or transmit analytics events to an external provider unless a provider is added later.

Service providers

Website infrastructure and configured processors may include Vercel for hosting, Supabase for inquiry storage, and Resend for email delivery. These providers process information to deliver their contracted services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or security, or as part of a business transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and requests

Inquiry information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for follow-up, business records, security, and legal obligations. Retention may vary with the nature of the inquiry and any resulting engagement.

To request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, or to raise a concern, email info@nouvexeng.com. Nouvex may need to verify the request before acting.
