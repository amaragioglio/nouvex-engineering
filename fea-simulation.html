FEA and mechanical analysis

Evaluate a design against defined loads, constraints, operating conditions, and acceptance criteria before committing to manufacturing or a design change.

Scope an analysisEmail Nouvex

Work in this route

The analysis method should follow the engineering question. Simulation can be paired with hand calculations and sizing checks where they add useful confidence.

  • Structural and stress analysis (FEA)
  • Thermal analysis
  • Fatigue and safety-factor review
  • Tolerance stack-up analysis
  • Hand calculations and component sizing

Define the model first

Useful inputs include geometry, material properties, load cases, restraints, contacts, temperatures, duty cycles, and the decision the analysis must support.

Results are interpreted within the agreed assumptions. They do not replace missing operating data, physical testing, or applicable certification requirements.
